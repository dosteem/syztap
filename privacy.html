1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy (“Policy”) explains how information is handled when you use the Syztap mobile application (“App”, “Service”).

We respect your privacy. Syztap is built so that core features — screen capture, cropping, and sending to an AI app you choose — happen on your device. We do not operate a Syztap backend that stores your screenshots or builds a profile of your activity.

2. No Account & Minimal Collection

  • No Syztap account. You do not need to create an account with us or provide your name, email, or phone number to use the core App.
  • No Syztap servers for captures. We do not run infrastructure to receive, process, or store your screenshots on our own servers.
  • No marketing analytics SDKs. The App does not embed advertising or product-analytics SDKs such as Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, Amplitude, or Adjust for behavioral profiling.
Note: Android, Google Play, and any payment provider may process limited technical or purchase-related data under their own policies when you install the App or buy a subscription. That processing is controlled by those platforms, not by a Syztap user database.

3. Data Processed Locally on Your Device

To power the floating button and capture flow, the App processes the following on device:

  • Activation state. Whether the overlay is enabled is stored in local app storage (for example SharedPreferences under a key such as syztap_activated) and is only readable by the App on that device.
  • Screen captures. Capture uses Android’s MediaProjection API only after you tap the edge control and accept the system permission dialog. Images may be compressed (e.g. JPEG) and written to the App’s temporary cache directory.
  • Retention of captures. Screenshots are temporary. They are intended to live only briefly (on the order of minutes) and are then deleted from cache. They are not saved to your permanent gallery by Syztap.
  • Clipboard. If you choose, text may be copied to the system clipboard for paste into another app.
  • Installed AI apps. The App may check locally which supported AI clients are installed so it can show available targets. That list is not uploaded to Syztap servers.

4. Sharing with Third-Party AI Applications

The only intentional recipient of a screenshot (or a cropped region) is a third-party AI application you select in Syztap (for example ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Grok, or Perplexity), provided it is installed on your device.

  • How sharing works. Transfer uses standard Android mechanisms such as a share intent (ACTION_SEND, image type) and/or a temporary content URI via a protected FileProvider. Data moves between apps on your device.
  • After handoff. Once the image is opened in the AI app, further processing is governed by that provider’s privacy policy (e.g. OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, xAI, Perplexity AI).

Syztap does not control and is not responsible for how third-party AI services use content after you send it.

5. Android Permissions

Depending on version and features, the App may require:

  • SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW (“Display over other apps”) — to show the edge button and overlay UI above other applications.
  • Foreground service related permissions (such as FOREGROUND_SERVICE, special-use or media-projection related types, and where applicable WAKE_LOCK) — so the overlay and capture pipeline can remain responsive under Android’s background limits, typically with a visible notification while active.
  • MediaProjection consent — a system dialog each time (or as required by the OS) before screen content can be read for capture.

You can revoke overlay permission in system settings; the floating button will stop working until permission is granted again.

6. Subscriptions & Payments

If you purchase Syztap Pro, billing is processed by Google Play. We do not receive your full payment card details. Google may share limited subscription status information necessary to unlock Pro features (for example via Play Billing and, if used, a subscription SDK such as RevenueCat).

Purchase history and refunds are managed under Google Play’s terms and privacy policy.

7. Children’s Privacy

Syztap is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data to us directly, contact us and we will delete it where applicable.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by a new effective date and, where appropriate, notice in the App or on our website. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated Policy.
