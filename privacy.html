Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Syztap Android application (“Syztap”, “the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information. It is designed to be clear and accurate.

Key point: Syztap does not operate its own servers for user content, does not create user accounts, and does not collect or store screenshots or the text of your questions on any systems controlled by the Developer. The App functions as a local bridge on your device between the screen content you choose to capture and the third-party AI applications you select.

Contact: info.dosteem@gmail.com

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, receive, or store:

  • names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other contact details;
  • account credentials or user profiles (the App has no user accounts);
  • screenshots or the text of questions you compose;
  • precise or approximate location data;
  • contacts, calendar, SMS, call logs, or microphone/camera content (beyond the system screen-capture permission you explicitly grant);
  • device advertising identifiers or other persistent device identifiers for tracking;
  • analytics, crash reports, or usage telemetry sent to us;
  • any data for advertising or marketing purposes.

We have no backend database that stores user-generated content from the App.

2. Information Processed Only on Your Device

The following information is processed and stored exclusively on your device using Android’s standard local storage mechanisms (SharedPreferences and the application cache directory):

Data Purpose Retention
Activation state (syztap_activated) Remember whether the floating edge button is turned on Until you change the setting or clear app data
Subscription status flag Enable Pro features in the overlay Until you clear app data or restore/change subscription
Daily ask counter and date Enforce the free-tier daily limit (10 requests) Resets automatically each calendar day; otherwise until cleared
Last selected AI model Remember your preferred model Until you change it or clear app data
Temporary JPEG screenshots in cache Preview and sharing to the AI app you choose Automatically deleted after approximately 10 minutes; also cleaned on new captures

You can delete all local data at any time via Android Settings → Apps → Syztap → Clear data / Clear cache, or by uninstalling the App.

3. Screenshots and Questions

When you activate the edge button:

  • Android shows the standard system MediaProjection consent dialog. Capture occurs only after you grant permission.
  • The resulting image is saved temporarily in the App’s private cache directory (JPEG, resized and compressed).
  • You may crop the image and type a question inside the overlay dialog.
  • When you tap “Ask”, the App uses standard Android sharing intents (ACTION_SEND) to deliver the image and/or text directly to the third-party AI application you selected (or opens its Google Play page if it is not installed). The content is handed over via a content URI with temporary read permission granted only to the receiving package.
  • As a fallback, the text of your question may also be placed on the system clipboard (labeled “Syztap”) so you can paste it yourself.

We never receive a copy of the screenshot or the question. Once the content leaves your device into a third-party application, that application’s own privacy policy and terms govern how the content is handled. We have no technical ability to access, view, or retrieve that content.

4. Subscriptions and Payment Data

Paid subscriptions are processed by Google Play Billing, facilitated by RevenueCat. We do not collect, store, or process your payment card details, billing address, or other financial information. Such data is handled exclusively by Google and RevenueCat under their respective privacy policies.

When you purchase or restore a subscription, technical identifiers necessary for subscription status (such as a RevenueCat-generated app user ID and purchase status) may be processed by RevenueCat and Google. This information is used solely to unlock Pro features and is not used by us for any other purpose.

Relevant third-party policies:

  • Google Play / Google Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy

The App can share content with the following applications (subject to which ones you have installed and which tier you use):

  • Claude (Anthropic) — package com.anthropic.claude
  • ChatGPT (OpenAI) — package com.openai.chatgpt
  • Gemini (Google) — package com.google.android.apps.bard

Each of these services has its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review them. Syztap does not control and is not responsible for their data practices.

6. Android Permissions

The App requests only the permissions necessary for its core functionality:

Permission Purpose
Display over other apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW) Show the floating edge button and Ask dialog on top of other applications
Foreground service (including special-use and media-projection variants) and Wake Lock Keep the overlay service running and support screen capture on modern Android versions
Internet and network state Required solely for Google Play / RevenueCat subscription verification and restoration
Google Play Billing Process and restore subscriptions

Screen capture itself is performed only after you explicitly approve the system MediaProjection dialog. The App does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, SMS, phone, or broad storage permissions. It does not use the QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES permission; it only checks for the specific AI packages listed above.

7. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information to us, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

8. Security

Local data is protected by the Android application sandbox. Temporary screenshot files reside in the App’s private cache and are automatically deleted after a short period. We do not transmit screenshots or query text to any server under our control. No method of transmission or storage is 100 % secure; you use the App at your own risk regarding the content you choose to capture and share.

9. Your Rights and Choices

  • Turn the overlay off at any time using the toggle on the landing screen.
  • Revoke the “Display over other apps” permission or MediaProjection consent through Android system settings.
  • Clear all local App data or uninstall the App to remove stored preferences and cache.
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions through your Google Play account settings.
  • Contact us at the email below with any privacy-related questions or requests.

Because we do not maintain user accounts or server-side personal data, certain rights that apply to stored personal data (access, deletion, portability) are exercised by you directly on the device as described above.

10. International Users

The App is designed for Android devices worldwide. Because we do not collect personal data on our servers, cross-border transfer of user content by us does not occur. Any transfer of content you initiate to a third-party AI application is governed by that service’s own terms and privacy policy.

Continued use of the App after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
